Handoff Note

A tiny instrument for one of the most fragile moments in work: handing context from one person or session to the next without losing what actually matters.

Moment

You stop, leave, switch devices, or ask another person to continue — and the real state of the work risks collapsing into vague summary.

Mechanism

Capture a handoff as a compact note with status, watch-outs, confidence, next move, and a witness-style trail of what changed.

Why it feels different

It is not a task app or a decision log. It is a transition artifact for continuity under interruption.
