Threshold Atlas

An instrument for watching uncertain signals approach the moment they become real enough to act on.

What it is

A temporal map of emerging signals, threshold crossings, and fragile recurrences.

Why it matters

Important decisions often happen too early or too late. This prototype tries to make the in-between visible.

Mechanism

Each signal is placed by confidence and recurrence, then traced as it accumulates toward a commitment threshold.

Temporal visualization, not dashboarding

Designed as an atlas: part map, part ledger, part sensing surface.

Color as signal, not decoration

Muted earth tones mark threshold pressure, uncertainty, and stabilization.

Single strong interaction

Select a signal to inspect how it changes shape before it becomes actionable.
